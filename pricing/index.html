    FAQ

    Pricing questions, answered.

    Do not see yours? Ask us below.

    How much does a website cost for a small business?

    Template shops advertise $199 to $549 but usually deliver a basic site and little support. Custom developers often charge $1,500 to $3,000 or more. At Clear Sites Web Agency a 5-page site starts at $500 to set up plus a monthly care plan, and you only add what you need.

    Can I buy just one thing?

    Yes. Every service here is sold on its own: the website, managed ads, Speed-to-Lead, AI booking, the Review Requester, a Lead CRM and local SEO. Pick what you need, or bundle several to save.

    Why is there a monthly fee?

    Because none of this is a one-time project. Monthly care covers hosting, security, edits, uptime checks and keeping your automations running. Without it, sites slow down, break and go stale.

    Do you run ads?

    Yes. We set up and manage Google and Facebook ads, with landing pages and call tracking. You pay the ad platforms directly for ad spend, and our monthly fee covers the management.

    Do you guarantee ad results?

    No. We build and manage your campaigns carefully and report results every month, but we cannot guarantee a specific number of leads, calls or sales. Results depend on factors outside our control, such as your market, budget, pricing, reviews, how quickly you respond to leads and how your business performs. This is spelled out in our written agreement.

    What does Speed-to-Lead do?

    When a customer fills out your form or misses your call, an automatic text and call-back reaches them within minutes, before they call someone else. Text messaging needs carrier registration, which we handle.

    What does the Review Requester do?

    After a job, it automatically asks happy customers for a Google review and flags unhappy ones for you to handle first. More reviews mean more trust and better local rankings.

    What is the Lead CRM?

    A simple, clean pipeline where every lead, job and follow-up lives in one place, set up for your business. We can also build a custom CRM or app if you need something specific.

    Do I own my website?

    You own your content, domain and business information. We host and maintain the site while you are on a plan, and we hand over your content if you leave. Ask for the exact terms before you sign.

    How long does it take to build?

    Most sites are ready for review within about a week once we have your logo, photos and services. Automations are added after the site is live.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Plans run month to month after setup. Ask us for the written terms.

    Do you build websites for contractors, landscapers and cleaners?

    Yes. We build for home-service and local businesses: contractors, roofers, landscapers, cleaners, auto shops, detailers, handymen, restaurants and more. Each site is built around your services and the towns you serve.

    Do you work with businesses outside Metro Detroit?

    Mostly we serve Metro Detroit and southeast Michigan, including Canton, Plymouth, Westland, Livonia, Romulus and Wayne, but we work with businesses anywhere. Call us or send the form.

    What is local SEO?

    Local SEO helps your business show up when people nearby search for what you do. It covers your website pages, your Google Business Profile, reviews and listings. Our sites come with the local SEO basics, and the monthly Local SEO plan keeps building on them.

    How is this different from a $199 website?

    Cheap template sites are a page and a goodbye. We build sites designed to bring in calls, connect them to automations that follow up on every lead, collect reviews for you, and keep everything maintained. The difference is leads, not looks.
