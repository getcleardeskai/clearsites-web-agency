FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Not here? Ask us or call and we will answer it.

How much does a website cost for a small business?

Our websites start at $500 to set up plus a monthly care plan that covers hosting, security and edits. See the pricing page for every option.

What is GEO, and do you offer it?

GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity to find, understand and recommend. We are rolling out an AI Search (GEO) service now. No one can guarantee an AI will name your business.

Can I buy just one service?

Yes. Websites, local SEO, ads, review requests, Speed-to-Lead and CRMs are each sold on their own, or you can bundle several to save.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or ad results?

No one honestly can. We do the work that gives you the best chance, report every month, and spell this out in our written agreement.

What areas do you serve?

We work with small and local businesses across the United States. We are based in Metro Detroit, Michigan, and we work by phone, video and email, so you do not need to be nearby. We also have local pages for Michigan towns.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most sites are ready for review in about a week once we have your logo, photos and services.

How much does a small business website cost?

Our websites start at $500 to set up plus a monthly care plan. The size depends on how many pages you need. See the pricing page for every option.

How long does it take?

Most sites are ready for review in about a week once we have your logo, photos and services.

Do I need to write the content?

No. Send us the basics about your business and we write the pages for you.

Will my site show up on Google?

We set up the local SEO basics on every site. Rankings take time and depend on your market and competition, and no one can guarantee a position.

How long does local SEO take?

Most businesses see movement in a few months, and bigger results over six to twelve. It depends on your market and competition.

Can you guarantee a first-page ranking?

No one honestly can. We do the work that gives you the best chance and report what happens.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

If you serve customers in person or at their location, yes. It is free and important for local results.

What is GEO?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. It is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants and AI search to find, understand and recommend, in the same way SEO helps you show up in Google results.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No. AI assistants decide for themselves, and they change often. We do the work that gives you the best chance and report honestly on what shows up.

Is GEO a replacement for SEO?

No. It builds on good SEO. A clear site, consistent listings, real reviews and helpful pages help in both places, so we do them together.

How soon will I see results?

Expect a few months. AI tools update on their own schedules, so changes do not show up right away.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Ad results depend on your market, budget, pricing, reviews and how quickly you respond to leads. We manage carefully and report every month, but we cannot guarantee a number of leads or sales.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Google or Facebook directly. Our fee is for managing the campaigns.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market. We recommend a budget in the first call based on your area and goals.

Is it allowed to ask for reviews?

Yes, asking customers for honest reviews is normal. We never write fake reviews and we do not offer incentives for positive ones.

How many reviews will I get?

It depends on your customers. Businesses that ask every customer usually collect far more than ones that do not.
