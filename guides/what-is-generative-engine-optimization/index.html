What Is GEO? How to Get Recommended by AI Search

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your business easy for AI assistants, like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI answers, to find, understand and recommend. It is similar to SEO, but the goal is to be named in the answer, not just listed in a row of links.

Why it matters for a small business

More customers are asking an assistant a direct question, such as "who is a good roofer near me" or "how much does a website cost", and reading a single answer. If your business is not part of that answer, those customers may never see you.

How AI assistants decide who to mention

No one outside these companies knows the full recipe, and it changes. In general, AI answers lean on information that is clear, consistent and easy to confirm from several places: your own site, your business listings, your reviews and mentions on other sites.

What you can do

  • State the basics plainly on your site. Who you are, what you do, where you work, what it costs and how to reach you, in short clear sentences near the top of your pages.
  • Keep your name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Your site, Google Business Profile and directories should all match.
  • Answer real questions. A page of honest questions and answers is easy for both people and AI tools to use.
  • Add structured data. It is code that labels your business, services and FAQs so machines read them correctly.
  • Let the crawlers in. Check that your site does not block the crawlers AI companies use, and consider adding an llms.txt summary file.
  • Earn real reviews and mentions. Reviews and mentions on other sites are signals that you are a real, trusted business.
  • Keep content fresh. Update pages when prices, services or hours change.

How to check how you look today

Ask a few assistants the questions your customers would ask, in your area, and write down who gets named. Repeat it monthly. That is a simple way to see whether your work is moving anything.

What no one can promise

No one can guarantee that an AI assistant will recommend a business. These tools decide on their own and change often. The work above helps, and it also helps in regular Google search.
