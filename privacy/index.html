Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Clear Sites Web Agency ("we", "us") is a business based in Michigan. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It covers two groups: visitors who use this website or send us a message, and clients who hire us for a website, ads, automations or a CRM.

What we collect

If you are a visitor or fill out a form: your name, phone number, email address, your business name and what it does, what you are interested in, any details you choose to write, and the page you sent the form from. We collect only what you type into the form.

If you are a client: your business and contact details, the content, logos and photos you give us, billing details handled by our payment provider, and information needed to build and run your website, ads or automations.

We do not use tracking pixels, advertising cookies or data brokers. This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message, prepare a quote and follow up with you.
  • To build, host and maintain your website, ads, automations or CRM if you become a client.
  • To keep records of our leads and customers in our own systems.
  • To send you a bill and take payment.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number or text-message opt-in data with third parties for their marketing.

Calls, texts and email

If you give us your phone number or email, we may call, text or email you about your request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Consent to be contacted is not a condition of buying anything. You can also ask us directly to stop contacting you.

Where it is stored and who helps us

We use a small set of service providers to run our business, and they receive only what they need to do their job:

  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • n8n (hosted on Railway) moves form submissions into our records.
  • Google (Google Sheets) stores a lead list.
  • Supabase hosts our customer database, which only our team can access.
  • Stripe handles payments. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Telnyx or a similar messaging provider delivers text messages when we use them.

Client customers' data

If we build an automation for a client, such as follow-up texts or review requests, we handle the client's customers' contact details on the client's behalf. The client decides how that information is used. If you are a customer of one of our clients and want your information changed or removed, please contact that business, or contact us and we will help.

How long we keep it

We keep lead and client records for as long as we need them to serve you and run our business, and we delete or correct them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Reply STOP to texts, or tell us to stop calling or emailing you.

To make a request, email getcleardeskai@gmail.com.

Security

We use reputable providers, private logins and access controls to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep it safe.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.
