FAQ

Review Requests questions.

Is it allowed to ask for reviews?

Yes, asking customers for honest reviews is normal. We never write fake reviews and we do not offer incentives for positive ones.

How many reviews will I get?

It depends on your customers. Businesses that ask every customer usually collect far more than ones that do not.

Does it need a website?

No. It works on its own or alongside your site.
